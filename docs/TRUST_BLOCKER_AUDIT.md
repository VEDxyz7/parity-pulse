## 1. Independent reference: exact current requirements

`services/trust_evidence.py::IndependentReferenceService.evaluate` requires:

- Token price exists; actual token source timestamp age is **0–120 seconds** at assessment;
  PRICE or PRICE_INFO kind, LIVE quality in LIVE mode, ingestion ≤ decision time. Exact ticker,
  issuer, chain, contract, symbol and ratio match metadata. Stock asset type is 1; ratio positive;
  metadata comes from BINANCE_RWA, was received within 120 seconds, and is not future-dated.
- Independent EquityObservation has positive price and non-null source time; correct ticker,
  LIVE mode, ingestion ≤ decision; current LIVE source allowlist is **MASSIVE only**.
- During calendar **REGULAR**, kind QUOTE or SNAPSHOT, quality LIVE, source age **0–120s**
  and absolute token/equity **source-time skew ≤30s**. Negative age rejects even when common
  transport quality permits a small clock tolerance. Binance RWA referencePrice is excluded.
- Outside REGULAR, the current rule uses REGULAR_CLOSE, HISTORICAL quality, interval 1minute,
  and bar start + one minute equal to the actual previous regular close. The closure age is
  reported; the 30-second current-quote alignment test is not applied to that historical close.
  Calendar handles holiday/early-close/DST boundaries, not unscheduled halts.

The adapter validates positive Alpaca bid/ask/sizes, non-crossed prices and RFC3339 source time,
preserves the original nanosecond string, and computes a Decimal **midpoint**, not a last trade
or official close. UNKNOWN/DELAYED feed status never automatically becomes LIVE. It does not
certify quote-condition suitability, a maximum spread or consolidated venue coverage. No new
spread threshold is invented here; those are source-quality questions for a later admission plan.

## 5. What 0/30, 0/30 and 0/3 actually mean

The denominators are **unchanged minimum safeguards**, not the total raw records inspected.
They apply to supported scopes, not global pooling across issuers, contracts, regimes or dates.

**Baseline (minimum 30):** TrustSample.scope includes mode, ticker, issuer, chain, contract,
regime, ratio, CURRENT/REGULAR_CLOSE and policy version. EpisodeBuilder groups completed
UTC 30-minute buckets by that scope. First observation must be within 120s of start; last
within 120s of end; internal gaps ≤120s. It selects the last feature at/before the middle and
an outcome from the end, never a future feature. Repeated timestamps do not add observations;
repository IDs prevent repeated windows. Eligibility requires past features, completed/available
outcome, same scope, 180-day lookback and exclusion of the current bucket. Baseline needs
≥30, no truncated input, and non-zero deviation standard deviation; degenerate history fails.
Trust saves a sample only with complete bounded news coverage, after reference/economics and
liquidity succeed. A sample can be collected while baseline classification is still insufficient.

**Opening model (minimum 30):** separate ResearchPolicy minimum, 180-day lookback and exact
scope including research/target versions. Decision must be off-hours, as-of verified, with
eligible features and a sufficient baseline. Outcome is
`(first_completed_5m_close - previous_regular_close) / previous_regular_close`, available only
after completion and verified revision provenance. Duplicate decisions/issuers do not create
independent stock openings: model eligibility deduplicates `(ticker, opening_at)` after scope
filtering. POSTHOC_ONLY is not AVAILABLE. ≥30 alone also cannot override missing features,
rank-deficient fit or other model validation.

**Analogues (minimum 3):** Trust AnalogueService uses eligible baseline episodes, sufficient
baseline scales and normalized feature cosine similarity ≥0.8, then top three; outcomes are
returned separately, not used in similarity. Research HistoricalRetrieval uses separately
eligible independent opening episodes, the same default similarity floor and configured top-K.
Its three matches are not simply three raw candles or three issuer copies of one opening.
The existing replay's `qualifying_analogues` counter reports maximum retrieval **eligible_count**,
not the number of above-floor selected matches; at zero the distinction does not change the
result, but a future positive gate check must inspect retrieved_count and status too.

The old isolated historical auditor deliberately marks unknown ratio/liquidity/as-of stages
false and outputs zero counts; that is a conservative missing-data assessment, not an episode
builder discovering zero after full inputs. **This audit independently verifies** the current
real source: zero stored TrustSamples/TrustEpisodes, 48 rejected ResearchDecisions, 48
POSTHOC_ONLY targets, zero eligible decisions/verified targets. The five rejection codes each
occur 48 times: ASOF_TOKEN_SHARE_RATIO_UNAVAILABLE, CALENDAR_VERSION_NOT_YET_VERIFIED,
KNOWN_PREVIOUS_REGULAR_CLOSE_UNAVAILABLE, KNOWN_TOKEN_PRICE_OR_METADATA_UNAVAILABLE and
REQUIRED_FEATURES_UNAVAILABLE. Some are cascading; they are not five disjoint sets of episodes.
The source reader has historical bars, but `known` rejects their later receipts at old decisions.

Therefore the last 0/30, 0/30, 0/3 readiness result is corroborated, not inferred merely from a
stale report. Current data cannot legitimately pass. Thirty-one full-span posthoc openings
across both issuers cannot be pooled into 30 eligible samples in an exact supported scope;
the earlier 180-day NVDA reopening scope had only 26 openings. Baseline 30-minute windows
can occur multiple times per day; opening-model independence is a different unit.

Minimum legitimate collection is: verified reference/ratio/liquidity/news and calendar context
known at each decision; dense completed 30m windows in each advertised baseline scope;
≥30 qualifying baseline episodes with nondegenerate statistics; ≥30 independent completed
and revision-verified opening outcomes per supported model scope within its lookback; then
≥3 eligible above-floor matches for each decision being evaluated. Enough opening outcomes
must occur after feature/baseline readiness, or come from independently verifiable archives.
No historical bar padding, current-ratio backfill, synthetic rows or copied issuer outcomes.
